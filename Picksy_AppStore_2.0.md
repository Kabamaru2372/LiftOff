## Description (EN)

**Most apps just show you your screen time. Picksy actually helps you cut it.**

You've seen the number. You know it's too high. Picksy is the screen-time app
that does something about it — and makes the whole thing feel good instead of
guilty.

**🛡️ Limits that actually work**
Set a daily time limit on the apps that eat your day. When you hit it, they lock
— even when Picksy is closed. No willpower required.

**⚔️ Duel your friends**
Challenge a friend: whoever has less screen time by midnight wins. Talk smack,
build streaks, climb your Picksy Score. Turns out, using your phone less is way
more fun together.

**📊 Honest numbers**
Picksy counts your real pickups and screen time — clear, no fluff — so you
actually know where your day goes.

**👨‍👩‍👧 Built for families**
Hand your kid the phone for a set time. When it's up, apps lock behind your
passcode — and only you can unlock another session.

**🌿 Be present**
Every minute off the screen is a minute back in your life. Picksy nudges you
toward it — gently, and with a sense of humor.

Less screen. More life. For real.

— Picksy. Be Present.
