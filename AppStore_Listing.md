## Description — English

Do you really need to be here right now?

LiftOff is a gentle phone awareness app that helps you notice — and break — the habit of mindlessly picking up your phone. No shaming. No guilt. Just a calm nudge that makes you pause and decide: do I need this, or am I just on autopilot?

HOW IT WORKS

Every time you pick up your phone, LiftOff detects it and shows you a simple question and a live timer. You choose: put it down, or continue. That's it. Over time, you'll start to notice your patterns — and change them.

WHAT YOU GET (FREE)

- Smart pickup detection
- Gentle nudge quotes in English & Greek
- Live timer showing how long you've been on your phone
- Daily stats: pickups and average time
- Weekly chart tracking your progress
- Streak counter for staying under your goal
- Live Activity on Lock Screen & Dynamic Island
- Customizable quiet hours and daily goals

LIFTOFF PRO (ONE-TIME PURCHASE)

- Heatmap: see exactly which hours are your worst
- Rewards: improve your weak hours and unlock badges, quote packs, and gift codes
- Quote packs: Stoic wisdom, humor, and philosophy collections (120+ extra quotes)
- Pay it forward: gift LiftOff Pro to a friend when you improve
- Monthly reports and trends

LiftOff was built to help you, not to monetize your attention. The free version does the job. LiftOff Pro is for those who want to go deeper.

No accounts. No cloud. No tracking. Everything stays on your device.

Made with care by Fotios Pongas.
