# Picksy — Positioning One-Pager (2.0 Rebrand Compass)

> Internal document. The single source of truth for name, tagline, App Store
> listing, onboarding copy, screenshots and visual direction. When in doubt about
> any wording or design choice, check it against this page.

---

## 1. The one-line truth

**Picksy helps you actually use your phone less — and friendly duels with friends make it spread.**

- **Hero (the message):** real behavior change — limits & shields that actually work.
- **Growth engine (the loop):** duels/challenges with friends → invite → compete → viral.
- **Soul (the philosophy):** *Be Present.*

The shields are **why it works**. The duels are **why it spreads**.

---

## 2. Positioning statement

> For **anyone who feels they're on their phone too much**, **Picksy** is the
> screen-time app that **actually changes the habit** — with real limits and
> friendly duels that make using your phone less feel *good*.
> **Most apps just show you your screen time. Picksy helps you cut it — with
> friends keeping you going.**

---

## 3. Audience

- **Primary:** anyone who wants focus / to be present — broad, not niche. People who've *tried* screen-time tools and bounced off because they were passive or joyless.
- **Secondary (own landing page, not the hero):** **parents** giving a child the phone for a set time, locked behind a passcode.

---

## 4. The three pillars

| Pillar | What it is | One-liner |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **1. Action / Δράση** (hero) | Accurate counting, app shields, daily time limits, friction nudges | *"It doesn't just track — it helps you cut it."* |
| **2. Friends** (engine) | Duels, Picksy Score, taunts, streaks | *"Less phone is more fun together."* |
| **3. Family** (secondary) | Time limits + passcode = hand the kid the phone, locked | *"Give the phone. Keep the control."* |

Everything we build/say should ladder up to one of these. If it doesn't, it's noise.

---

## 5. Taglines

**Primary candidate:** `Use your phone less. For real.`

**Alternates (by mood):**
- Punchy: `Beat your screen time.`
- Warm: `Less screen. More life.`
- Social: `Put it down — together.`
- Philosophy/sub-brand: `Be Present.`

EN / GR / DE for the lead tagline:
- EN: *Use your phone less. For real.*
- GR: *Λιγότερο κινητό. Στ' αλήθεια.*
- DE: *Weniger Handy. Wirklich.*

---

## 6. Value props (customer-facing copy)

1. **It actually works.** Set limits and shields that hold — even when the app is closed. *(Δουλεύει στ' αλήθεια — όρια που κρατάνε.)*
2. **Less phone, with friends.** Duel friends, climb the score, talk smack. *(Λιγότερο κινητό, με φίλους.)*
3. **Honest numbers.** Counts your real pickups & screen time, no fluff. *(Τίμια νούμερα.)*
4. **For families.** Hand your kid the phone for a set time — locked with a passcode. *(Για οικογένειες.)*

---

## 7. Tone of voice

- **Encouraging, not preachy.** We're a teammate, not a scold.
- **Playful but honest.** Taunts and humor, but never fake or guilt-trippy.
- **Direct.** Short sentences. Real talk. ("For real." — not "optimize your digital wellness journey.")
- **Human.** Talks about *life off the screen*, not "metrics."

---

## 8. Differentiation — why Picksy, not the rest

| Competitor type | Their weakness | Picksy's edge |
|-----------------|----------------|---------------|
| Passive trackers (Apple Screen Time, etc.) | Show a number, change nothing | Acts: shields + limits that cut it |
| Blockers (Opal, one sec, ScreenZen) | Solo, joyless, easy to ignore | Social duels make it fun + sticky |
| Parental apps | Built for control, not the user | Same app works for *you* AND your family |

**The unfair combo:** *real behavior change + social fun + family-ready,* in one friendly app.

---

## 9. What Picksy is NOT (guardrails)

- ❌ Not "all-in-one digital wellbeing" (says everything, means nothing).
- ❌ Not a pure analytics dashboard.
- ❌ Not a guilt machine.
- ❌ Not led by the parental angle (it's a strong *secondary*).

---

## 10. Name

**Keep "Picksy."** Catchy, friendly, ownable. We refresh the *meaning* (from
"counts pickups" → "win back your attention"), not the name. Renaming would throw
away existing ASO/recognition for no gain.

Sub-brand line stays: **Picksy — Be Present.**
